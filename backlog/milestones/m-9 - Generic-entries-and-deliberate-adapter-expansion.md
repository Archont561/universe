## Description

After the Conda producer and Airlock consumer contracts are real, broaden the
catalogue deliberately: generic content-addressed artifacts first, then Agent
Skills, Python, and STAC only as dogfooded native contracts justify first-class
adapters. The five-adapter budget from decision-13 remains binding.

Exit condition: at least one non-Conda universe is dogfooded end to end through
publication and offline consumption without weakening the static canonical
output or evidence model.
